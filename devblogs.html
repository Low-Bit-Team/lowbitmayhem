Devblog #1 - The First Released Devblog

Written by SA64 - Apr 30, 2025

Hello everyone and welcome to the first of what should hopefully be a consisent release of Devblogs for Low-Bit Mayhem on the new Low-Bit Mayhem website. These devblogs will contain updates to the project and what's happening behind the scenes.

I had started to write a devblog about a month ago containing stuff about the April Fools update and the stuff that happened before and after the update, but I procrasted on finishing it that what was written in it isn't as relevant anymore. I left that devblog below this one if you wanted to read it.

Now, without further ado, here's pretty much everything of note for Low-Bit Mayhem.

April Fools Update

About a month ago, we released a new update that promised Psych 0.7.3 Compatibility, Updated Art, and the removal of some features. However, if you were astute about the date, you'd realize that it was an April Fools Update. I even hinted at it with "Hope you enjoy this update and don't forget to check your calendars (and your time zone if you don't live in America)". Despite that, one of the promised features were true, it was compatible with Psych 0.7.3.

The version of Bit-Beat that was included in the update was actually an older version of the song that originally meant to release with NKO, a different mod directed by my friend ShultzNG. I'm not gonna explain the whole story here, just play the update if you want to learn the lore.

Nothing from the update will be in v2 when it releases, it's a one-and-done thing, except for...

Bit-Beat (Pico Mix)

As teased in the update, as well as on Twitter and Bluesky, Bit-Beat is getting a Pico Mix! This was the calayst for me wanting to kickstart devlopment of a Low-Bit Mayhem V2.

My original idea for a Bit-Beat (Pico Mix) was to go with a completely different style, as opposed to the existing SMB1 style. I thought it would be cool to instead do a Sonic style. I'll let you figure out why I wanted to do that style in particular. Fortunately, Cryoptera was able to get the style pretty much exactly what I wanted. Hope you're excited to see what the song will look like when it's finished.

Hope you enjoyed the April Fools update, here's a lil teaser of an upcoming song that was thrown in to that update! Song by @cryoptera.bsky.social Art by @shultzyng.bsky.social
